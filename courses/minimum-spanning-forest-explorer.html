Minimum spanning forests,
one edge at a time.

Choose the cheapest cycle-free connections that span every original connected component. Follow Kruskal’s choices, keep isolated vertices visible, and see why some links must be rejected.

A minimum spanning tree minimizes the total chosen edge weight. It does not generally preserve every shortest route. If the input is disconnected, the result is a forest.

1. Enter your graph

Vertices are named A onward, up to H. A vertex with no edge remains in the graph.

Undirected, simple graph: no self-loops and no repeated unordered pair. Negative and zero weights are valid.

Use A B -3: two vertex letters and an integer from −99 to 99, separated by spaces or tabs. Blank lines are allowed. A–B and B–A are the same unordered pair.

Editing either input retires the previous trace.
