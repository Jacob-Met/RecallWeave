What remains at equilibrium?

Predict a direction, account for every component, and inspect the composition that balances one reversible reaction.

A + B ⇌ C

One ideal homogeneous mixture. Fixed temperature and volume. Activity coefficients are 1. This lab predicts composition; it supplies no reaction time.

Use a = [A]/c°, b = [B]/c° and c = [C]/c°, with c° = 1 mol L⁻¹. The displayed concentration ratios, Q = c/(ab), and K are dimensionless.
