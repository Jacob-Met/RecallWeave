      A question to carry forward

      One label can hide two routes.

      For Aa × Aa, choose each cell that produces Aa. One parent can supply A and the other a, or the contributions can be reversed.

      Now compare the genotype and phenotype summaries. Why do the two summaries group the same cells differently?

      Try AABb × Aabb next. Derive its probabilities from its own gametes before reaching for a familiar ratio.

      What this model assumes — and what it leaves out

        Linked-locus gamete weights, incomplete dominance, interactions between genes, environmental effects and survival differences require a suitable model. The course includes questions about these boundaries. This explorer stays within the assumptions listed here.
