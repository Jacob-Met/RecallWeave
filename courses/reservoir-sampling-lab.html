A sample as the stream grows

Keep a fixed number of occurrences while arrivals continue. Follow a chosen Algorithm R history, then inspect the exact counts across every possible history.

This lab makes no random draws. You choose the integer draws for the trace. Distribution tables separately assume independent, uniform draws from 1 through the incoming position.

    Scope and assumptions

    The bounded lab enumerates at most 40,320 histories per input. Positions identify occurrences, even when labels repeat. It does not generate random numbers, estimate unknown distributions or guarantee representative categories in a particular sample. The method can process a continuing stream without knowing its final length; the bounded enumeration here is a teaching aid.
