Does the condition change the probability?

For a nonempty table, independence means P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B). Compare the two integer products exactly:

Count(A and B) × total
Count(A) × Count(B)

Empty or certain events can satisfy this identity even when some conditionals are undefined. This describes your finite table; it does not establish causation or independence in an outside population.
