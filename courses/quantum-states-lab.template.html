Amplitudes can cancel

For a = r + i·s, its probability is r² + s². H combines amplitude pairs as ((a+b)/√2, (a−b)/√2). Equal probabilities before H can hide different signs or phases, so they need not predict equal probabilities afterward.

Marginals leave information out

For q0 = 1, add probabilities at 10 and 11; for q1 = 1, add 01 and 11. A Bell state and |++⟩ have the same 50/50 marginals, yet different joint tables. One probability table alone cannot certify entanglement.
