What this model can establish

Strict, complete, equal-size one-to-one preferences only. The completed result has no blocking pair, and every proposer gets the best partner available to that proposer in a stable matching. Stability does not promise uniqueness or fairness; ordinal ranks are not utilities. No ties, incomplete lists, capacities, strategic behavior or real allocations are modeled. The worked guide includes original examples and mathematical references.
