The lab models a stipulated recombinant-gamete fraction in a hypothetical `AaBb × aabb` testcross. It preserves exact rational probabilities and distinguishes chromosome phase, genotype, parental classes and recombinant classes. The guide explains why matching the independent-assortment distribution at 50% does not determine chromosome location.
