02 · The mapping

Keep the answer key explicit

  • Each record is one question. Keep the exact template header names; their order may change.
  • Supply option_1 and option_2. Add up to option_6; leave unused trailing option cells empty.
  • correct_option is numbered 1–6. A value of 2 means option_2.
  • prerequisites may be empty, or a JSON array such as ["observation"]. Names must match a concept in the same CSV exactly.
  • Quote cells containing commas, quotes or line breaks. Double a quote inside a quoted cell.

Question order, choice order, wording and IDs are kept. Concepts follow their first appearance. The check validates the deck structure and links; review your content and answer key yourself.

Up to 100 questions, 32 concepts and 256 KiB of UTF-8 CSV. The resulting JSON must also fit the existing 256 KiB deck limit.
