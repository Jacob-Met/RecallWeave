Names and prerequisites

Enter 1–8 unique names, one per line, with 1–32 Unicode characters per name. Names are case-sensitive. Keep arrows out of names.

One arrow per line: A -> B or A → B. Use exact names from above. Blank means no dependencies. List each arrow once; a self-loop is allowed.

Blank lines and outer spaces are ignored. Tabs and control characters are refused. The full draft is limited to 8 KiB of UTF-8 text. Editing clears the active plan and its progress.
