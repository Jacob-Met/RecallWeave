Watch the gap, then the energy

The remaining voltage gap is multiplied by exp(−t/τ). After 1 τ, about 36.8% remains; after 5 τ, about 0.674% remains. A small gap at finite time is still a gap.

vC = Vs + (V0 − Vs) e^(−t/τ)
i = (Vs − V0) e^(−t/τ) / R

Source work = ΔU + resistor heat

Source work is positive for delivery and negative for absorption. The residual is work − ΔU − heat; tiny values can reflect floating-point rounding. Display values are rounded; JSON uses full numerical precision.
