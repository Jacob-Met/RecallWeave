    Three things to try

    1. Keep Even number of 1s. Trace 1011, then the empty word. Explain why they have different outcomes.
    2. Compare it with Every binary word. Before pressing Compare, predict the shortest word that separates them.
    3. Load Ends with 01 and trace 010. An accepting prefix is not enough. Try appending one more symbol.
