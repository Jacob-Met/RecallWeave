What this check can tell you

Matching dimensions permit a scale conversion for the listed units. They do not prove that a physical equation is correct, determine a numerical constant, or tell you what kind of quantity is being described.

For example, energy and torque share the same base dimensions but describe different quantities. Interpret your calculation in its physical context.

Arithmetic treats your entered number as exact. It does not estimate measurement uncertainty or select significant figures from an experiment. Affine temperatures, logarithmic units and arbitrary formulas are outside this explorer.

Supported symbols and exact scales

Use explicit multiplication: m*s means metre × second; ms means millisecond. Division proceeds from left to right: kg/m*s differs from kg/(m*s). μm also accepts µm or the typed spelling um; ², ³ and · are accepted conveniences. Other symbols are not inferred.
