Read the convention

Edges are what we cover.

  • Undirected edges may be used in either direction. Loops count twice toward degree and once toward traversal.
  • Only edge-bearing components must connect. Isolated vertices need not be visited.
  • A circuit has at least one edge here. With no edges, we report a separate zero-edge walk.
  • A wrong requested start does not mean the whole graph has no Euler trail.
