Inputs, exact arithmetic and execution limits

The start accepts integers, decimals with up to three decimal places, or fractions with a positive denominator at most 1000 and numerator magnitude at most 20000, within −20 to 20. The polynomial has four integer coefficients from −12 to 12. Choose 1–8 steps.

Every fraction and stopping decision is exact within a 4096-bit numerator/denominator bound. A resource stop preserves the last complete step and establishes no convergence or divergence. Plot coordinates and short decimal labels are approximate. Editing an input retires the previous record until you run again. This self-contained page makes no network requests.
