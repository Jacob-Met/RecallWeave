These are authored mathematical curves. A point gives an instantaneous velocity; the area over an interval gives a change in position.

What does editing change?

Any point edit clears the previous result. Apply the whole curve to calculate again. Nothing is saved automatically or sent anywhere.

Try a zero between the ticks

Set two points: (0 s, 1 m/s) and (1 s, −2 m/s).

The velocity crosses zero at 1/3 s. At the end, net displacement is −1/2 m, but distance is 5/6 m.

Explain why taking the absolute value of the final displacement would miss some of the travel.
