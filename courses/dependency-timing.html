How to read these times
Earliest start and finish
A job starts after all its prerequisites finish. Its finish is its start plus its duration.
Latest start and finish
The latest schedule keeps the whole plan’s earliest finish unchanged, including every terminal job.
Total slack
Latest start minus earliest start. It measures this job’s allowable delay while preserving the project finish; connected jobs cannot always spend their slack independently.
Critical job
A job with zero total slack. More than one branch may be critical, and a zero-duration milestone can be critical too.
