From a download to a lesson

  1. Extract the ZIP.Keep the RecallWeave folder and its files together.
  2. Open catalog.html.Use a web browser, then choose Open learner to open the matching learner.
  3. Choose your course.Under Bring your own lesson, choose a JSON file from the included courses folder. Inspect the preview, then select Start this deck.

Together in the folder

Everything for this learning flow.

The pack contains the catalog, its matching learner, the original registered course files and opening instructions. Courses keep their source and permission statements.

Once extracted, the catalog and learner work without a server, account or internet connection. JavaScript must be enabled. Use the learner’s download controls when you want to keep a study record.

Looking for one course? Browse individual downloads. The pack covers the catalog and learner; interactive labs and study tools are available separately.

RecallWeave’s model estimates are illustrative. They are not grades or evidence of learning efficacy.
