    What the proof says

    A feasible assignment gives a value you can achieve. A cut gives a value you cannot exceed. When they meet, the maximum is known.

    This explorer uses Edmonds–Karp: each augmentation follows a path with the fewest residual edges. It visits neighbors in the entered vertex order, preferring forward use before cancellation to the same next vertex, then original edge order. It does not choose by largest bottleneck or smallest cost.

    All capacities and flows here are exact small integers. The network is a teaching model; its units and behavior are not measurements of pipes, traffic or another physical system.
