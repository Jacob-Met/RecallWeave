Exactly what counts as one unit?

One new-element write costs 1. Copying one old live element during a resize costs 1. Allocation, zeroing, bytes, cache behavior and elapsed time are excluded. These are teaching-model counts, not measurements of a language runtime or machine.

When full, doubling chooses capacity 1 initially and then twice its old capacity. Fixed growth adds the chosen increment. This lab models appends only: no removals, shrinking or middle insertion.
