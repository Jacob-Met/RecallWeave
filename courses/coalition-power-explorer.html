Two questions, two denominators

Absolute swing probabilityplayer's swings / other-player coalitions

Assumes every other player independently chooses yes or no with equal probability. These values need not sum to one.

Normalized Banzhaf shareplayer's swings / all players' swings

A share of the counted swings. These values sum to one; that does not certify fairness or predict behavior.

Fractions are exact and reduced. Percentages are decimal approximations; the bars use the same 0–100% scale for each measure.
