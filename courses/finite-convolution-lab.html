Why a matching-index dot product is different

Convolution pairs x[k] with h[n−k]. For real lists, the separately defined zero-lag correlation r[0] pairs x[k] with h[k]. For x = [1, 2] and h = [3, 4], convolution gives y[0] = 3, while r[0] = 11. Always state the index convention.

Model, source, and attribution

This lab uses finite real lists and full zero-extended linear convolution. It does not wrap indices, crop the result, simulate a continuous signal, or process a recording. Decimal input has four-place precision; output is displayed to at most eight places. All examples are authored teaching inputs.
