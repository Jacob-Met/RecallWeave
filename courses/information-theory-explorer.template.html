Does either label tell you anything?

Independence requires every joint probability to equal the product of its marginals. The exact test is cell count × total = row total × column total. Rounded bits never decide it.

Inspect the terms, including the negative ones

Entropy averages surprise. Mutual information averages the log ratio between a joint probability and the corresponding independent probability. An individual signed information term can be negative even though the total is nonnegative.

Why very small information needs careful arithmetic

Subtracting almost equal entropies or adding positive and negative terms can lose tiny differences. The total uses an equivalent divergence expression that avoids that cancellation. The signed table still shows the original p × log2(p/q) terms. The complete observation includes both decompositions. See the worked guide for the identity; exact integer checks alone decide independence.
