Shown values are rounded to three decimal places. In the exact geometry, the residual's dot product with the line direction is zero.

Check both parts

The projection must lie on the line, and b minus that point must be perpendicular to the line. A perpendicular arrow pointing the other way does not reconstruct b. For a nonzero direction a, the coefficient is (b · a) / (a · a).
