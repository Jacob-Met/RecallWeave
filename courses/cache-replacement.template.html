      Read the rule before the result

      A hit finds the requested page already present. A miss loads it; if all slots are occupied, one page must leave first.

      FIFO keeps the insertion order on a hit. LRU refreshes the hit to most recent. The highlighted page is the request you just inspected.

      Neither rule knows future requests. LRU's capacity guarantee compares LRU with itself at different capacities; it does not make LRU the winner on every workload.
