01 / Set up a curve

Choose your points

Two to four x,y pairs. Whole coordinates from −20 to 20. Separate points with lines or semicolons.

Use 0, 1, or a fraction such as 1/3. The numerator may not exceed the denominator; both are at most 1000.

Changing an input retires the displayed calculation until you apply it.
