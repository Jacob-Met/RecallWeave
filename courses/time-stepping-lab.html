What this experiment can and cannot show

A decaying numerical sequence need not be accurate. To compare refinement, hold the physical endpoint fixed: use h = 1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 with N = 1, 2, 4, 8 at T = 1.

This bounded scalar example uses finite floating-point calculations. It does not prove general convergence, identify a physical device or establish improved learning outcomes. A negative initial value changes the sign of the state, not the multiplier classification.
