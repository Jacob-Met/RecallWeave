The distinction to test

For 0 ≤ r ≤ 1/2, the weights are nonnegative and sum to one: each new value stays within the old range. At r = 1/2 an alternating pattern flips without shrinking. Beyond that boundary, its magnitude grows even though its sum stays fixed.

A constant field remains constant for every admitted ratio. One calm example cannot establish stability for all initial fields.

This dimensionless discrete teaching model corresponds to r = κΔt/Δx² in an explicit heat-equation discretization. It provides no material fit or continuous-solution error estimate.
