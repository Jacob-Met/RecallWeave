### Prepare a course from CSV

Open [Question bank to course](csv-course.html) to turn a local CSV question bank into a checked lesson JSON. Download the template, supply your questions and numbered answer key, enter the title and source credit, and review the complete course before downloading. The saved JSON opens in the existing Deck studio and learning app. The [column guide](docs/CSV_COURSES.md) explains exact headers, optional choices, prerequisite names and limits.
