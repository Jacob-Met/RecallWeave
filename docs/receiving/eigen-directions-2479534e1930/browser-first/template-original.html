Exact check for this probe

For a nonzero input v = (x, y) and output Av = (u, w), the two vectors are collinear exactly when xw − yu = 0. This test includes a zero output.

Read the equation carefully

Av = λv, with v ≠ 0

The line is preserved. The arrow may keep its direction, reverse it, or collapse to the origin. The zero vector is never an eigenvector.

Why repeated roots need another check

Subtract the eigenvalue from both diagonal entries and solve (A − λI)v = 0. A shear with a repeated root leaves one eigenline; a scalar matrix leaves every line. Multiplicity and eigenspace dimension answer different questions.

The model covers real 2×2 integer matrices within the displayed bounds. Its integer probes cannot exactly represent an irrational slope. Complex eigenvalues are reported symbolically; their eigenvectors are outside this real-plane drawing.
