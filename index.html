How the adaptive model works

Bayesian Knowledge Tracing estimates mastery per concept using explicit initial-knowledge, guess, slip, and learning-transition probabilities. After each response, the selector scores unasked items by expected information gain, weighted toward prerequisite concepts. The values are illustrative defaults, not a validated assessment.
