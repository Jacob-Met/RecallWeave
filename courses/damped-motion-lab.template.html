Model and limits

The damper force −bv opposes velocity; the spring force −kx opposes displacement. Mechanical energy E = ½mv² + ½kx² obeys dE/dt = −bv². No drive, dry friction, collision or nonlinear effect is included.

Regimes use the exact entered ζ: zero is undamped, values strictly between zero and one are underdamped, exactly one is critical, and values above one are overdamped. Critical and overdamped trajectories may cross equilibrium under adverse initial velocity. A settling claim needs a tolerance and initial conditions.
