Inspect the blocks behind a prefix sum. Add to one value, follow the entries that change, and combine two prefixes into an inclusive range sum.

1. Choose values and operations

Each tree entry i covers [i − lowbit(i) + 1, i]. Here lowbit(i) is the largest power of two dividing positive i.

1–16 comma-separated integers, each from −99 through 99. A leading + or − and decimal leading zeros are accepted; fractions, exponents and empty entries are refused.

add INDEX DELTA adds −99…99; prefix END sums 1…END, with END 0 allowed; range START END sums the inclusive interval. Use at most 64 operations. An empty script shows only the initial tree.
