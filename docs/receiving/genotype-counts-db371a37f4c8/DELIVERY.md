## What the lesson distinguishes

The examples are hypothetical diploid plants at one autosomal locus with allele labels A and a. Direct copy accounting requires no equilibrium assumption. Model genotype probabilities require separately stated independent gamete draws from pools with the same allele fractions; persistent Hardy–Weinberg behavior requires additional generational assumptions.

The public G1..G6 tables include an odd-sized group with fractional expected counts, two groups with the same allele totals but different genotype pairings, an all-AA boundary and an empty group whose observed fractions are undefined. Observed counts remain observations; fractional model expectations are not rounded into fictitious plants. A discrepancy is not a biological diagnosis or significance test, and an exact numerical match does not prove the assumptions.

This is original authored teaching material, not a clinical resource, a real-population assessment or a claim of educational efficacy. The guide identifies the actually consulted primary/institutional references and the limits of the source reading. Original wording and examples are CC0; cited works retain their terms.
