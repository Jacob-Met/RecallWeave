Mathematical conventions and original content

For z = a + bi, Re(z) = a and Im(z) = b. Nonzero multipliers scale distance by their modulus and add their argument modulo 360°. The zero multiplier collapses a point to the origin; zero itself has no direction. P₀ is assigned directly, so a zero-step sequence does not require 0⁰.
