01 / 03 · From counts to a model

Keep the record and the prediction separate

For a nonempty group, N = nAA + nAa + naa. Each plant has two copies. For N > 0, p = (2nAA + nAa)/(2N) is the A-copy fraction, and q = (2naa + nAa)/(2N) is the a-copy fraction. Record the observed genotype fractions first. Then, separately, stipulate independent union of gametes from two pools with the same p and q: model probabilities are p², 2pq and q². Expected counts use the stated cohort size M; keep fractions exact.
