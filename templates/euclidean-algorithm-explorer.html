Why the answer survives

Keep the common divisors.

If A = qB + r, a number that divides A and B also divides r. A number that divides B and r also divides A. Both pairs therefore have the same common divisors.

Every new remainder lies from zero up to—but excluding—the divisor. That shrinking nonnegative integer guarantees a stopping point.
