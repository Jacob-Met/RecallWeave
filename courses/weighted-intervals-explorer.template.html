    Assumptions, tie rules and what the model leaves out

    Activities have fixed start/end times, require one shared resource, and contribute independent nonnegative values. You can select any compatible subset, including none. There are no setup gaps, dependencies, multiple rooms or uncertain durations. The page maximizes the stated sum of values, rather than the number of activities or time occupied.

    Rows are ordered by end, start and exact ID. When take and skip are equal, the current row is skipped. This returns one deterministic optimum; another schedule can have the same best value. The earliest-finish schedule is shown as a comparison heuristic for total value and may tie or fall short. The full algorithm uses sorting and predecessor search; this explorer caps inputs at eight activities to keep every step readable.
