    What this can tell you.
    
      
    
        
    You can inspect how explicit assumptions and hypothetical responses produce an estimate. The same update function powers the lesson; this walkthrough does not reproduce the adaptive question selector or infer anything about a person.
    
        
    The defaults are illustrative, not fitted to you or validated as an assessment. The model treats knowledge as two hidden states, allows a learning transition and has no forgetting term. A high estimate does not establish understanding, retention or transfer.
    
      
    
      
    Background: Corbett & Anderson, Knowledge tracing: Modeling the acquisition of procedural knowledge (1994). The paper's findings do not validate this demo's defaults. The walkthrough itself works offline; opening the reference is optional.
